Housework Champion Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Housework Champion (the "App") takes your privacy seriously. This policy explains what information the App uses, where it is stored, and what is processed in connection with ads.

1. Information we collect

The App has no sign-up or login, and does not collect or send your information to the developer's servers.

  • What you enter in the App (your title, chore lists and times, meals, fridge items, shopping list, notes, study time, etc.) is stored only on your device.
  • The developer cannot view this content and does not share it with or outsource its processing to any third party.
  • No analytics tools are used. The font is bundled in the App, so it does not connect to external servers.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a banner ad at the bottom of the screen and rewarded ads that you choose to watch.

  • To serve ads, Google may collect and use your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID), device information and ad interaction information, for ad delivery and ad performance measurement.
  • The App does not pass what you enter (chores, meals, notes, etc.) to advertising companies.
  • You can turn off personalized ads, or reset or delete your advertising ID, under Settings > Google > Ads on your device.
  • For how Google handles data, see policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

3. Permissions

  • Internet access: used to load ads.
  • Notifications: if you turn them on, the App schedules reminders on your device to tell you chore times. Reminder content does not go through any server.
  • The App does not request sensitive permissions such as location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

4. Retention and deletion

  • What you enter is stored on your device and is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App. The developer cannot restore deleted content.
  • Files created with the App's backup feature are kept by you and are not sent to the developer.

5. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the changes and the effective date on this page and in an app update.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, please email: gsycontact@gmail.com
