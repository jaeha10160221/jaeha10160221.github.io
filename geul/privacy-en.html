Geul & Write Privacy Policy

Effective: November 2, 2026 · Contact: gsycontact@gmail.com

1. Information we collect

The app has no accounts and does not collect names, emails or location. Your works, notes, settings, photos and music files stay on your device and are never sent to the developer.

2. Ads

The app shows a Google AdMob banner ad. AdMob may collect and use the advertising ID, IP address, device information and app usage data to serve and measure ads; see Google's policy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

3. Fonts

Some fonts may be downloaded from Google Fonts, in which case Google receives ordinary connection data such as your IP address.

4. Backups

Backup files are created only when you export them and are saved where you choose.

5. Retention and deletion

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes everything. The developer holds no user data.

6. Children

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over and does not knowingly collect children's data.

7. Changes

Updates will be posted on this page.
