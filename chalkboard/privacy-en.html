School Chalkboard Privacy Policy

한국어English日本語

Effective date: October 21, 2026

School Chalkboard (the "App") does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number or location. There is no sign-up or login.

1. Information stored only on your device

  • Board drawings, schedules and settings (language, theme, volume, etc.) are stored only on your device and are never sent to the developer.
  • Uninstalling the App deletes this information.
  • "Save / share as image" and "Save board file" only save to your device or share to an app you choose, when you choose to do so.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • The App shows a Google AdMob banner ad at the bottom of the screen.
  • Because the App is used by children, every ad request is tagged as child-directed. No interest-based (personalized) ads or remarketing are used, and ad content is limited to a General (G) rating.
  • Google may process limited technical information, such as IP address and device type, to show ads and prevent fraud. The advertising ID is not transmitted for child-directed ad requests.
  • Learn more: Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from partner apps

3. Permissions

  • Internet: to display ads.
  • Vibration: to vibrate when a bell rings (can be turned off in Settings).
  • The App does not access the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photo library.

4. Children's privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Parents or guardians with questions can contact us below.

5. Sharing with third parties

The App collects no personal information, so none is shared. Advertising-related processing is described in section 2.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version and its effective date will be posted on this page.
